TripKhata privacy policy

Effective 10 November 2026

The short version

  • Your trips are saved on your phone. Nothing leaves it unless you sign in and share a trip.
  • A shared trip is stored in Google Firebase so the people in it can see and edit it. Only people who joined that trip can read it.
  • No ads, no tracking for marketing, and we never sell or rent your data.
  • You can delete your account and data from inside the app at any time.

Who can see your data

Trips that are not shared stay only on your phone. A shared trip can be read and edited by the people who joined it with its code. The data is processed by Google Firebase on our behalf (Firebase privacy information), and trip photos by Cloudflare R2 (Cloudflare privacy policy). We don't share it with anyone else, unless required by law.

How long it is kept

Data on your phone stays until you delete it or uninstall the app. A shared trip stays in Firebase until its creator deletes it for everyone, or until nobody is left in it. Push tokens are removed when you sign out or delete your account.

Delete your account and data

  1. Open TripKhata → Profile → Settings.
  2. Under your Google account, tap Delete account and confirm with Google.
  3. Tick "Also erase all trips and my profile from this phone" to remove everything locally too.

This removes you from every shared trip, deletes your push tokens and deletes your sign-in. Trips that nobody else is in are deleted completely, including bill photos. In trips you shared with friends, the expenses stay for them (they are the group's record), but they are no longer linked to your account.

To delete a single shared trip for everyone, open it → ⋮ → Delete for everyone (trip creator only). To remove everything from the phone, uninstall the app.

Can't open the app? Ask for deletion through the contact link below and we'll do it within 30 days.

Children

TripKhata is not meant for children under 13 and we don't knowingly collect their data.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. Questions or deletion requests: open an issue on GitHub.
